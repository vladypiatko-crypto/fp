# 🦆 Quack Attack!

A pixel-art **survival shooter** for **1–4 players**: split screen on one device, or **online with friends** through lobbies. You're a duck whose weapon **aims all by itself**. You just move, dodge and dash.

## Story

**Chapter 1: Save Princess Daisy.** Princess **Daisy** of Puddle Pond has the legendary **Golden Quack**, a voice so pure it can power any machine. The evil goose scientist **Dr. Honk** swoops in with his airship, kidnaps her to power his **Mega Honk Mech**, and leaves his robot army behind to keep the ducks busy.

**Chapter 2: The Stolen Quack.** A week later, at Daisy's thank-you party, Dr. Honk is back in his giant **Mothership**. This time his tractor beam steals only her voice, leaving Daisy croaking. The ducks chase the ship from the beach to the moon to get the Golden Quack back.

**Chapter 3: The Robot Rebellion.** Dr. Honk now runs a bakery at Puddle Pond, but his old robots built themselves a new boss out of scrap: **King Clank**. He kidnaps Honk and wants to turn the whole world into one big factory. The ducks chase him over a volcano, through the desert, a candy factory and the crystal caves, up to Clank Castle.

**Chapter 4: Lost in Time.** Dr. Honk's new **Time Toaster** opens a hole in time, and out flies **Tick-Tock**, a robot clock who wants to rule all of time. He scatters the ducks through history: a dino jungle, a pirate cove, a knight's keep and the wild west, until they reach his home, Neon City 3000.

**Chapter 5: Save the Pond!** One morning Puddle Pond is empty: the **Kraken-Bot** is pumping all the water in Duckland down to its undersea factory. With Dr. Honk's snorkels the ducks dive after it, all the way down to Atlantis.

**Chapter 6: Mecha-Quack.** In the old scrapyard all the broken robots from chapters 1 to 5 rattle together into **Mecha-Quack**, a robot duck that wants to be the better duck. The ducks chase it through a robot circus, a glowing mushroom forest and into cyber space, up to its Mecha Fortress.

## Features

- **Online with friends**: host a lobby and share its 4-letter code, or join a friend's. Up to 4 ducks, each on their own device. No server to set up: devices connect directly. Pick story or endless, ready up, and start.
- **Lots of weapons**: Blaster, Quackshot, Bubble Gun, Feather-rang, Beak Beam laser, Quackinator minigun, Corn Cannon (pops into popcorn), Frost Blaster (slows robots), Firework Launcher (homing rockets that explode), Tesla Coil (chain lightning), Dragon Breath flamethrower, Egg Launcher (lobbed exploding eggs), Saw Launcher (saws bounce robot to robot), Duck Sniper (one huge shot through a whole line), Black Hole (sucks robots in, then pops), Mine Layer, Quack Sword (melee slashes), Prism Laser (three rainbow beams), Ninja Stars, Bee Hive (homing bees), Harpoon Gun, Meteor Staff (meteors from the sky), the limited event weapons (Halloween, Christmas, Easter, Valentine's, Summer, New Year), and the admin-only Golden Quacker and Ban Hammer
- **Level-up cards**: gun and duck upgrades (multi shot, piercing, speed, regen, dash attack…) plus **10 power-ups**: egg orbit, bread bombs, chain lightning, mega quack, feather darts, splash puddles, bubble shield, duck turret, sun beam and frost nova. More gun cards (big bullets, lucky shot crits, eagle eye range) and duck cards (tough feathers, bookworm, lucky coins, vampire bite, second wind). You get **3 power-up slots**; once they are full only those three come back. Eggs block bullets and push robots away.
- **Overcharge**: after a power-up is maxed, a rare red glowing OVERCHARGE card can show up and makes it much stronger (giant fast-spinning eggs, splitting bread storms, lightning that jumps to everything, lava lakes, mirror shields…).
- **Robot army**: Bolt Bots, Boom Bots, Goo Blobs, Tank Bots, Zap Drones and Saw Bots, each with its own behaviour. Bosses telegraph charges, jumps, bullet rings and missile strikes.
- **Impact frames**: big hits freeze the world for a moment, flash in inverted black and white with speed lines, then a shockwave.
- **Soundtrack**: every world has its own chiptune song (beach, swamp, sky, space, factory…), plus boss, chapter finale and locker tunes.
- **Honks and quacks**: Dr. Honk honks, the ducks quack. Both are synthesised in the browser, no sound files.
- **Locker**: customise each duck's **skin, hat, halo, trail and weapon**. Every hat sways, sparkles and has its own little extras (twinkling crown, floating notes, chef steam).
- **Chests**: spend the coins you earn on Duck Chests, Royal Chests or the **Mega Chest** (900 coins, only epic and legendary items). **Unlocks are shared by both players.** New items get a bouncing red NEW! on their locker tab and a red ! on the item until you tap it.
- **Shop** (SHOP button in the locker), more things to spend coins on:
  - **Training**: permanent upgrades, 5 levels each, more expensive every level: Tougher Duck (+10 max HP), Sharper Beak (+6% damage), Faster Feet (+4% speed), Bigger Magnet, Quick Learner (+5% XP) and Piggy Bank (+10% coins from games). Training also works online; every duck brings their own.
  - **Daily deals**: 4 items you don't have yet, to buy straight away. New ones every day.
  - **Boosters** for one game: Double Coins, Head Start (2 free upgrade cards) and Extra Life. Switch them on and they're used at the start of your next game, online too.
  - **The admin decides what's in the shop** (admin tab → SHOP): pick up to 8 items as special offers with your own prices (instead of the random daily deals), and set the Admin Sale price and booster prices. Saved for every player at once.
- **Boss Rush** (BOSS RUSH on the title screen): all 30 story bosses one after another, with only a few robots in between. After each boss you heal up and get 2 free upgrade cards. Your record is how many bosses you beat.
- **Pets** (new PETS tab in the locker, found in chests): little buddies that follow your duck and help: Baby Chick (bigger magnet), Piglet (finds coins), Froggy (tongue zaps), Mini Drone (lasers), Ninja Cat (ninja stars), Fairy (heals), Baby Dragon (exploding fireballs), Robo Pup (fast lasers), and the admin-only Golden Goose.
- **Event coins**: while a holiday event runs, every game also pays purple EVENT COINS (more for winning the event level). The event chests (Pumpkin, Present, Easter, Love, Beach, Party, Lantern, Lucky) cost 30 event coins instead of normal coins.
- **Quests** (QUESTS button on the title screen, with a red number when rewards are waiting):
  - **Daily quests**: 3 new quests every day (scrap robots, beat bosses, survive in endless, get a combo…) paying coins, plus a bonus for finishing all 3
  - **Achievements**: 24 big goals (scrap 10000 robots, finish every chapter, survive 20 minutes, own 100 items…), each with a coin reward to claim
  - **Stats**: everything you've ever done, from robots scrapped to time played
- **Daily reward**: coins every day you open the game; the streak grows for 7 days (500 coins on day 7)
- **Combo counter**: kills close together build a combo, with an announcer at 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 (QUACKTASTIC!, QUACKPOCALYPSE!…)
- **Elite robots**: rare bigger golden robots with a crown, 4x the HP and much better loot
- **Vibration**: phones buzz a little when you get hurt (switch it off in settings)
- **Settings** (gear icon): turn off screen shake, impact flashes or music, pick your **language** (English, Deutsch, Русский, or QUACK, where every word is QUACK) and set your **name**.
- **Your own name**: type any name up to 12 letters and numbers. Rude names (English, German and Russian, including l33t spellings) are refused, and the host filters friends' names too.
- Ducks turn in 4 directions, blink, squash & stretch and leave afterimages when dashing.

## Downloads

**Updates:** every build knows its own build number. When the game starts it checks GitHub for a newer build; if there is one, an UPDATE screen pops up. On Android, UPDATE downloads the new APK inside the game and opens the installer (it installs over the old version and keeps your save), then tap Open. On Windows the game downloads the new exe, closes, puts it in place of the old one (no second copy) and starts it again; your save stays. CLOSE leaves the game.

Every push builds the apps (GitHub Actions → **Build game apps**) and publishes them on the **Releases** page:

| File | Runs on |
| --- | --- |
| `QuackAttack-Windows.exe` | Windows 10/11, portable, double-click to play (SmartScreen: *More info → Run anyway*) |
| `QuackAttack-Android.apk` | Android phones/tablets (allow "install unknown apps") |
| `QuackAttack.html` | Any web browser |

Online play works in all three too, and they can play together (an Android phone can join a Windows PC's lobby).

## Online play

Choose **ONLINE**, then **HOST A GAME** on one device. It shows a 4-letter code. Friends choose **JOIN A FRIEND** on their own phone or PC and type that code. Up to 4 ducks, each on their own device. The host picks story or endless and starts.

### How it works

There is no game server to run. The free public [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com) service introduces the devices to each other (the host registers its lobby code there), and from then on they talk directly over WebRTC. On networks that block direct connections, PeerJS's free relay carries the traffic instead.

The **host's device runs the real game**. Everyone else sends their moves and button presses to the host and draws the host's game about 12 times a second. So the host's device has to stay open and connected for the whole match, and everybody's lag is roughly their connection to the host.

### Limits

- Every device needs internet, also when you're all on the same Wi-Fi (the matchmaker lives online).
- No public lobby list: you join by code only.
- If the host closes the game, locks the phone or loses connection, the lobby ends for everyone. A friend whose device goes quiet for about 12 seconds is dropped.
- Online depends on the free PeerJS service being up. If it's down, local play still works.
- For testing you can point the game at your own PeerJS server with `?peer=host:port` in the address.
- Set `ONLINE_READY = false` in `www/js/online.js` to hide online play behind an "under construction" sign.

## Accounts and the admin tab

With Firebase switched on, every player picks a **name** and a **unique username** on first start (needs internet once). The admin account gets an **ADMIN** button on the title screen: search players by username, **flag** them (3 flags = automatic ban), **ban / unban**, **reset progress**, and send gifts from the **gift tab**: every item in a grid (admin items first, owned ones marked), coin amounts that add up, pick as many as you like and send them together with an optional **message** (or a message alone). Players get an animated **ADMIN GIFT!** popup that shows what they got and the message. Gifts can go to one player or **to everyone** at once, and the admin tab shows how many players there are. Items with the rainbow **ADMIN** rarity (wizard hat, Galaxy skin, Admin Halo, Admin Sparks trail, Golden Quacker, Ban Hammer) only exist as admin gifts. In a game, the admin's pause menu has **ADMIN POWERS**: heal everyone, level up, clear robots, spawn the boss, god mode.

**More admins:** the first account is the **OWNER**. The owner can open any player in the admin tab and tap **MAKE ADMIN** (or **REMOVE ADMIN**); that player gets the admin tab straight away. Other admins can do everything except make or remove admins, and nobody but the owner can flag, ban or reset the owner. The admin tab marks the OWNER and ADMINs in the player list.

**News:** the NEWS button in the admin tab sends a message to every player. It pops up once for each of them (as NEWS! from the admin) the next time they're on a menu, also for players who were offline when it was sent. The gift tab's COINS page also has **event coins** to give away.

Setup (once):

1. Create a project at [console.firebase.google.com](https://console.firebase.google.com), add a **Web app**, turn on **Authentication → Anonymous** and create a **Firestore Database** in production mode.
2. Put the web app's `firebaseConfig` into `FIREBASE_CONFIG` in `www/js/cloud.js` and set `ACCOUNTS_ON = true`.
3. Paste the whole of `firebase/firestore.rules` into **Firestore Database → Rules** and press **Publish**. The rules are what stop anyone else from using admin powers, even with an edited app. (Paste them again whenever this file changes: events like Halloween need the newest rules.)
4. **The first account ever made becomes the admin (the owner)**, for good. So the admin installs the build and creates their account before anyone else.

Good to know: accounts are anonymous and live in the app's storage. Uninstalling or clearing the app's data starts a new account (the old username stays taken), and a banned player could get a fresh account that way. Without `FIREBASE_CONFIG` the game skips accounts and runs fully offline.

Testing locally: `npx firebase-tools emulators:start --only auth,firestore` in `firebase/`, then open the game with `?emu=1` (the first account you make there is the admin).

## Controls

| | Player 1 | Player 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Touch | drag anywhere on **your half** = joystick, tap **DASH** (or tap with a 2nd finger) | same, on the other half |
| Keyboard | WASD + Space | Arrow keys + Enter |
| Gamepad | 1st controller: stick/D-pad + A | 2nd controller |

Online, each device controls its own duck with the controls above (keyboard players use the first set). Menus work with touch, mouse, arrows + Enter/Esc, or a gamepad. Pause with ⏸ / Esc / Start.

## Project layout

- `www/js/cloud.js`: accounts, bans, flags, resets and admin gifts (Firebase) · `www/js/admin.js`: the admin tab · `firebase/firestore.rules`: who may change what
- `www/js/lang.js`: translations (all text is written in English and translated as it's drawn), the QUACK language and the name filter
- `www/js/engine.js`: canvas scaling, pixel font, sprites, chiptune and voice audio, input, UI widgets, impact frames, network state, save file
- `www/js/art.js`: all pixel art (ducks, hats and their animation, robots, bosses, worlds)
- `www/js/data.js`: cosmetics, weapons, enemies, bosses, levels, dialog
- `www/js/game.js`: gameplay (the host runs this; online friends run the same code path but only draw)
- `www/js/online.js`: peer-to-peer connections, the host's lobby, online menu and room screen, snapshots and the online game
- `www/js/vendor/peerjs.min.js`: [PeerJS](https://github.com/peers/peerjs) 1.5.5 (MIT)
- `www/js/story.js`: dialog boxes and cutscenes · `www/js/chapter2.js` to `chapter5.js`: chapters 2 to 5 · `www/js/halloween.js`, `christmas.js`, `holidays.js`: the events (worlds, bosses, levels, rewards, bonus events)
- `www/js/scenes.js`: title, map, locker, chests, settings
- `www/js/shop.js`: the shop (training, daily deals, boosters) and the Mega Chest art
- `main.js`: Electron wrapper (Windows) · `android/`: Capacitor project · `scripts/build-single-html.js`: one-file web build

Run locally by opening `www/index.html` in a browser, or with `npm install && npm start` for the desktop app.
